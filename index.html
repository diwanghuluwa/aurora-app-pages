Aurora

A personal assistant app for one person.

Aurora is Richard's personal assistant app. It is built for his own use and is not offered to the public.

What it does with Google Drive

Aurora keeps the files he gives it (for example documents he asks it to translate, and the results) in one place on his own computer. If he chooses to connect Google Drive, Aurora stores a copy of those files in a folder it creates itself in his Google Drive, so he can open them from any device.

  • Aurora asks for a single Google permission, drive.file. With it, the app can only see and change files and folders that Aurora itself created in his Drive. It cannot see any of his other files.
  • The connection can be removed at any time from his Google Account (Security, Third-party apps with account access).

How the app handles information is described in the privacy policy.

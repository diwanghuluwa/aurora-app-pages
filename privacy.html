Aurora — Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Aurora is a personal assistant app built for one person, Richard, for his own use. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data when he connects his Google Drive. See the home page for what the app is.

What Aurora accesses

Aurora requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With this permission the app can access only the files and folders that Aurora itself creates in his Google Drive. It cannot see, read, or change any other file in his Drive.

How it is used

Aurora uses this access for one purpose: to keep a copy of the files he gives it (and the files it produces for him) in a folder it creates in his own Google Drive, and to notice when he changes, moves, or deletes those copies there. It does nothing else with Google user data.

Where data is stored

The files and the sign-in credentials stay on his own computer and in his own Google Drive. They are not copied to any other service operated by the app. The credentials (an access token and a refresh token issued by Google) are stored on his own server and are not shared.

Sharing and disclosure

Aurora does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to anyone. Nobody other than him has access to the app or to the data it handles. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train machine-learning models.

Compliance

Aurora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in his Drive until he deletes them (in Drive, or through the app). He can disconnect Aurora at any time by removing its access in his Google Account (Security, Third-party apps with account access), and can delete the folder Aurora created. Removing access stops the app from reaching his Drive immediately.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support email address shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
